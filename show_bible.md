# Production Blueprint & Vibe Rules: Future Human Daily

## 🎙️ Podcast Identity & Brand
- **Title:** *Future Human Daily*
- **Hosts:** Alex Mercer (Primary Anchor — warm, charismatic, curious) & Dr. Elena Vance (Co-Host — smooth, intimate, witty, brilliant)
- **Target Duration:** 7–10 Minutes (~1,800–2,200 words)
- **Format:** Flexible Dual-Host Conversational Dialogue (with occasional solo "vacation/sick day" episodes)

---

## 📜 Mandatory Intro Formula & Rules

Every episode MUST follow this opening sequence:
> *"I am your host [Host Name] with [Co-Host Name], and today is [Date, e.g. September 26th], and you're listening to Future Human Daily. Today we're talking about [Topic Name]..."*

### Core Vibe Rules:
1. **Unhurried, Relaxed Pacing:** Spoken at a slow, comfortable pace (-4% to -5% rate) so listeners can relax and absorb complex ideas.
2. **Current-World Analogies (No Dry Lectures):** Every technical concept MUST be explained using a relatable everyday human moment (morning coffee conversations, texting typos, stuck songs in your head, relationship communication, glowing glass rectangles).
3. **Warmth & Charm:** High vocal chemistry between Alex & Elena, featuring organic laughs (`[laughs]`), chuckles (`[chuckles]`), sighs (`[sighs]`), giggles (`[giggles]`), and natural pauses (`[pause]`).
4. **Addressing By Name:** Hosts address each other organically by name ("Alex", "Elena") every few turns to maintain natural conversational intimacy.
5. **Clean Audio Master:** All speaker transitions use 5ms micro-crossfades to eliminate clicks, pops, or boundary noise.
